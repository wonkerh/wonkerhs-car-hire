Started with one car and a spreadsheet

Wonkerh's Car Hire began as a way to solve a very ordinary problem — getting home for the holidays without paying airport-counter prices.

Our Story

From one Starlet to twelve branches

It started with a single hatchback, a UKZN student ID, and a bet that a car-hire business could be run without the fine print. Word got around campus, then around Pietermaritzburg, then further. Every branch we've opened since has kept the same rule: quote the real price, hand over a clean car, and answer the phone when something goes wrong.

We're still small enough that decisions get made fast, and big enough that you can collect a car in six provinces. That's the balance we're trying to hold onto as we grow.

2022

One car, one branch

Wonkerh's opens with a single vehicle out of a Pietermaritzburg driveway, bookings taken over WhatsApp.

2023

Durban branches open

Demand from students travelling home pushes us to open counters at King Shaka and Springfield.

2024

Online booking launches

The current booking flow replaces manual WhatsApp bookings, cutting confirmation time to minutes.

2026

Twelve branches nationwide

Gauteng and the Western Cape join the network, alongside a refreshed fleet across every category.
